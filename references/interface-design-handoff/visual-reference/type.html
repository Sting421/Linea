Type

Two faces, both self hosted as WOFF2 and loaded from the bundle's fonts folder.

Two faces

The display face carries identity, magnitude and every numeral. The body face carries prose. Opening Hours Sans is a single weight, so never ask it for bold.

RoleFaceFileWeightSpecimen

The type scale, ten tokens

Sizes are rem, so they scale with a user's own text size. The pixel column is at a 16 pixel root and is for reading only. Titles, labels and eyebrows take the display face. Body, body small and caption take the body face.
