Ovanova design system

A static snapshot of the design system, built from the merged tree of the fifteen open pull requests plus the two equipment tests. Every page opens straight from a file, no server is needed. 43 components, 31 with written previews and 12 showing a floor card.

TokensStatus, categorical, brand and ink colour, radius, shadow and spacing.TypeThe two faces and the ten type tokens.

A floor card means no preview has been written for that component yet. Its page shows the placeholder card the bundle uses, not the component in use.

Shell, header and plant header

The shell, the header and the plant header are page containers in the repo, under app/containers/Layout and app/containers/Plant/PlantHome/PlantHeader, and are not part of this bundle. The one component here is the error screen.
