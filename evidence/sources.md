## Safety threshold references, checked 3 October 2026

The following public guidance informed the current safety rules. Linea's
notification tiers, conservative fallbacks, and clarification limits are
product decisions, not a clinically validated scoring system. UK contact
numbers are source context and must not be copied into Philippine scripts.

| Source | Relevant evidence |
| --- | --- |
| [NHS: Falls](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/falls/) | Inability to get up and possible head, back, neck, or hip injury warrant emergency help. |
| [NHS: Shortness of breath](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shortness-of-breath/) | Severe breathing difficulty and associated chest or consciousness changes are emergency indicators. |
| [NHS: Chest pain](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/chest-pain/) | Persistent or spreading pain and pain with breathlessness, sweating, sickness, or lightheadedness warrant emergency help. Resolved/recurrent pain still warrants medical advice. Linea's all-current-pain Emergency rule is a stricter product fallback. |
| [NHS: Dizziness](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/dizziness/) | Persistent/recurrent dizziness and associated symptoms warrant medical review. |
| [NHS: Stroke symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/) | Sudden neurological signs may require emergency help even after resolution; guidance explicitly includes signs in the last 24 hours. Linea does not diagnose stroke. |
| [NHS: Fainting](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/fainting/) | Recovery and circumstances matter; incomplete recovery, associated serious symptoms, exertional or supine fainting require emergency help. |
| [NHS SPS: Missed or delayed doses](https://sps.nhs.uk/articles/advising-on-missed-or-delayed-doses-of-medicines/) | Omission risk and advice vary by medicine; time-critical medicines need specific handling. No generic missed-dose safety claim is appropriate. |
| [NHS: Losartan](https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/losartan/) | Medication-specific information directs dosing questions to professionals and excess doses to urgent advice. Linea records answers but does not reproduce dosing instructions. |
| [NHS: Poisoning](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/poisoning/) | Suspected harmful ingestion requires immediate medical help; symptoms can be delayed. |

No study validating Linea's tiers, classifier accuracy, or outcome safety is
claimed. Existing demographic evidence and provider behavior were not
re-verified as part of this threshold work.
